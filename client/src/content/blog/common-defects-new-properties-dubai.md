# 15 Most Common Defects Found in New Properties in Dubai

When purchasing a new property in Dubai, whether it's an off-plan apartment in Downtown or a ready villa in Arabian Ranches, buyers often assume everything will be perfect upon handover. However, our experience conducting thousands of snagging inspections across Dubai reveals that even premium developments from reputable developers frequently have defects that need addressing. This comprehensive guide outlines the most common issues we encounter and explains why professional snagging is essential for Dubai property buyers.

## Why New Properties in Dubai Have Defects

Before examining specific defects, it's important to understand why brand-new properties in Dubai often have issues:

1. **Rapid Construction Schedules**: Dubai's ambitious development goals sometimes lead to accelerated construction timelines.

2. **Multiple Subcontractors**: Large developments involve numerous subcontractors, creating quality control challenges.

3. **Climate Challenges**: Dubai's extreme heat affects materials and construction methods.

4. **Labor Skill Variations**: Different skill levels among construction workers can impact finishing quality.

5. **Final Inspection Limitations**: Developers' internal quality control may miss issues that specialized snagging inspections identify.

## The 15 Most Common Defects in New Dubai Properties

### 1. Uneven Wall and Ceiling Finishes

**Description**: Visible undulations, uneven application of plaster, or inconsistent paint finish on walls and ceilings.

**Impact**: While primarily aesthetic, these issues affect the overall appearance and can worsen over time as settling occurs.

**Example Locations**: Most commonly found in large wall expanses and ceiling corners.

**Detection Method**: Visual inspection with oblique lighting and straight-edge tools.

### 2. Tiling Defects

**Description**: Hollow-sounding tiles (indicating improper adhesive application), uneven tiling, lippage (height differences between adjacent tiles), and grout issues.

**Impact**: Beyond aesthetics, improperly installed tiles can crack, come loose, or allow water penetration.

**Example Locations**: Bathroom floors, kitchen backsplashes, and main living areas.

**Detection Method**: Tapping tiles to identify hollow sounds, using straight edges to check alignment, and detailed visual inspection.

### 3. Door and Window Alignment Issues

**Description**: Doors and windows that don't open or close properly, have uneven gaps, or rub against frames.

**Impact**: Affects daily functionality and can worsen over time, potentially leading to security issues or weather infiltration.

**Example Locations**: Bedroom doors, bathroom doors, balcony doors, and windows, especially in larger openings.

**Detection Method**: Operational testing of all doors and windows, gap measurement, and alignment checks.

### 4. Plumbing Defects

**Description**: Water pressure issues, leaking connections, improperly sealed fixtures, slow drainage, and toilet flushing problems.

**Impact**: Can lead to water damage, increased utility bills, and potentially serious structural issues if left unaddressed.

**Example Locations**: Bathroom fixtures, kitchen sinks, washing machine connections, and water heaters.

**Detection Method**: Functional testing of all water outlets, drainage speed tests, and inspection under sinks and behind fixtures.

### 5. Electrical Faults

**Description**: Non-functioning outlets, switches controlling incorrect fixtures, missing outlet covers, and improperly installed light fixtures.

**Impact**: Presents both inconvenience and potential safety hazards.

**Example Locations**: Throughout the property, especially in kitchens and bathrooms where multiple electrical points are concentrated.

**Detection Method**: Testing all outlets and switches with appropriate equipment, checking fixture installation.

### 6. HVAC (Air Conditioning) Issues

**Description**: Insufficient cooling, unusual noises, improper installation of units, condensation problems, and thermostat malfunctions.

**Impact**: Given Dubai's climate, AC issues significantly impact comfort and can lead to increased energy consumption.

**Example Locations**: All air-conditioned spaces, with particular attention to larger rooms.

**Detection Method**: Performance testing of all units, noise assessment, condensate drainage inspection.

### 7. Waterproofing Failures

**Description**: Inadequate waterproofing in wet areas, leading to water seepage and potential structural damage.

**Impact**: One of the most serious defects, as water damage can be extensive and expensive to repair after the warranty period.

**Example Locations**: Shower areas, around bathtubs, balconies, and flat roof sections.

**Detection Method**: Moisture meters, water testing of shower areas, and inspection of adjacent areas for signs of seepage.

### 8. Balcony and Terrace Defects

**Description**: Poor drainage, inadequate slopes, cracked tiles, and insufficient waterproofing.

**Impact**: Can lead to water pooling, leaks into the property or units below, and premature deterioration.

**Example Locations**: All exterior spaces, especially larger balconies and terraces.

**Detection Method**: Water testing, slope measurement, and inspection of drainage systems.

### 9. Kitchen Cabinet Installation Problems

**Description**: Misaligned doors, hardware issues, improperly secured cabinets, and countertop installation defects.

**Impact**: Affects daily usability and can worsen over time with regular use.

**Example Locations**: Kitchen cabinetry, especially in larger kitchen layouts.

**Detection Method**: Operational testing of all cabinets, alignment checks, and inspection of mounting security.

### 10. Marble and Stone Defects

**Description**: Unpolished areas, chipped edges, inadequate sealing, and improper installation of stone surfaces.

**Impact**: Primarily aesthetic but can affect the durability and maintenance requirements of these premium finishes.

**Example Locations**: Flooring, countertops, and decorative elements.

**Detection Method**: Detailed visual inspection, surface testing for proper sealing.

### 11. Parking Area Issues

**Description**: Incorrect parking space allocation, inadequate clearance, problematic access control, and improper lighting.

**Impact**: Affects daily convenience and potentially property value.

**Example Locations**: Building parking facilities for apartments and villa garages.

**Detection Method**: Verification against contract specifications, functional testing of access systems.

### 12. Smart Home System Malfunctions

**Description**: Non-functioning home automation features, connectivity issues, and incomplete setup.

**Impact**: Reduces the value and convenience of premium technology features.

**Example Locations**: Throughout smart-enabled properties.

**Detection Method**: Systematic testing of all smart features and integration points.

### 13. Noise Transmission Problems

**Description**: Insufficient sound insulation between rooms or from external sources.

**Impact**: Significantly affects comfort and potentially property value, especially in apartment buildings.

**Example Locations**: Between bedrooms, bathrooms, and neighboring units.

**Detection Method**: Sound testing under various conditions.

### 14. Appliance Installation Issues

**Description**: Improperly installed built-in appliances, missing features, and functionality problems.

**Impact**: Affects daily usability and potentially voids manufacturer warranties.

**Example Locations**: Kitchen appliances, built-in washers/dryers, and smart home appliances.

**Detection Method**: Operational testing of all appliances through their full functionality range.

### 15. External Fixtures and Fittings

**Description**: Loose railings, improperly secured light fixtures, and defective external features.

**Impact**: Presents both aesthetic and safety concerns.

**Example Locations**: Balcony railings, external lighting, and architectural features.

**Detection Method**: Physical testing of security, visual inspection, and verification against specifications.

## What to Do If You Find These Defects

If defects are identified in your new Dubai property:

1. **Document Everything**: Take detailed photos and notes.

2. **Report Promptly**: Submit all issues to the developer within the warranty period (typically 1 year).

3. **Prioritize Issues**: Focus on functional and safety concerns first.

4. **Follow Up Diligently**: Maintain a record of all communications.

5. **Verify Fixes**: Don't sign off on repairs until they're properly completed.
