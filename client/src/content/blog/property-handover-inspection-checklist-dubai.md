# Complete Property Handover Inspection Checklist for Dubai Properties

Accepting the keys to your new property in Dubai is an exciting moment, but it's also a critical time to ensure your investment is defect-free. This comprehensive property handover inspection checklist will help you navigate the snagging process in Dubai, whether you're purchasing an apartment in Dubai Marina, a townhouse in Arabian Ranches, or a luxury villa in Palm Jumeirah.

## Why a Detailed Handover Inspection is Essential in Dubai

Before diving into the checklist, it's important to understand why a thorough property inspection is particularly crucial in Dubai:

- Properties in Dubai can range from AED 800,000 to several million dirhams, making this one of your largest investments
- The 1-year defect liability period starts from handover, not from when you identify issues
- Dubai's climate creates unique challenges for buildings that may not be immediately apparent
- Fixing issues after the liability period expires can cost tens of thousands of dirhams

## Pre-Inspection Preparation

Before you or your professional snagging company begins the inspection, ensure:

- **Utilities are connected**: Electricity, water, and AC should be functional
- **All areas are accessible**: Including storage rooms, utility closets, and roof spaces
- **Developer's representatives** are aware of the inspection
- **Bring the right tools**: Measuring tape, spirit level, plug tester, flashlight, and camera
- **Have floor plans and specifications** to verify against what's delivered

## Exterior Inspection Checklist

### Building Façade and Structure
- [ ] Exterior walls free from cracks, stains, or uneven coloring
- [ ] Balcony railings secure with no rough edges
- [ ] External drainage systems properly installed
- [ ] No water staining or evidence of leaks
- [ ] Exterior lighting functional and properly sealed
- [ ] Building access systems (gates, doors, etc.) working correctly

### Landscaping and Common Areas (for Villas and Ground Floor Units)
- [ ] Irrigation systems properly installed and functioning
- [ ] Plant material healthy and as specified
- [ ] Paving even with no trip hazards
- [ ] External lighting operational
- [ ] Boundary walls and fences secure and as specified
- [ ] Pool equipment functioning (if applicable)

## Interior Inspection Checklist

### General Interior Checks
- [ ] Ceiling height matches specification
- [ ] Walls and ceilings free from cracks and uneven surfaces
- [ ] Paint finish consistent with no visible marks
- [ ] Floor levels even throughout with no creaking or movement
- [ ] Skirting boards properly attached and aligned
- [ ] All specified light fixtures installed and working
- [ ] No water stains on ceilings or walls
- [ ] Room dimensions match the floor plan (within acceptable tolerance)

### Doors and Windows
- [ ] All doors open and close smoothly without scraping
- [ ] Door frames square and properly aligned
- [ ] Door hardware (handles, locks, stoppers) functioning
- [ ] Windows open, close, and lock properly
- [ ] Window seals intact with no gaps
- [ ] No condensation between double-glazed windows
- [ ] Window screens installed if specified
- [ ] No scratches on glass surfaces
- [ ] Balcony doors have proper weatherproofing

### Flooring
- [ ] Tile work even with consistent grout lines
- [ ] No hollow-sounding or cracked tiles
- [ ] Marble/stone surfaces properly sealed (if applicable)
- [ ] Wood flooring properly installed with no gaps or buckling
- [ ] Carpet properly stretched and secured (if applicable)
- [ ] Floor transitions smooth between different materials
- [ ] No stains, chips, or damage to any flooring

## Electrical Systems Checklist

### Electrical Outlets and Switches
- [ ] All outlets and switches installed as per plan
- [ ] All electrical points functioning
- [ ] No loose outlets or switches
- [ ] Cover plates properly aligned and secure
- [ ] GFCI outlets in wet areas (kitchens, bathrooms)
- [ ] Light switches controlling correct fixtures

### Lighting and Fixtures
- [ ] All light fixtures installed and working
- [ ] Recessed lighting properly aligned
- [ ] Light fixtures secure with no visible gaps
- [ ] Ceiling fans balanced and working on all speeds (if applicable)
- [ ] Dimmer switches functioning properly (if applicable)

### Electrical Panel and Systems
- [ ] Distribution board properly labeled
- [ ] Circuit breakers functioning
- [ ] No exposed wiring
- [ ] Smoke/fire detection systems operational
- [ ] Internet and TV points installed as specified

## Plumbing Systems Checklist

### Bathroom Fixtures

- [ ] Sinks drain properly with no leaks
- [ ] Showers and tubs drain properly
- [ ] Water pressure adequate at all fixtures
- [ ] Hot water available and at proper temperature
- [ ] No leaks under sinks or around fixtures
- [ ] Shower doors/screens properly installed and sealed
- [ ] Tile work around wet areas properly sealed
- [ ] Grout lines consistent and sealed
- [ ] Exhaust fans operational

### Kitchen Plumbing
- [ ] Sink drains properly with no leaks
- [ ] Garbage disposal functioning (if applicable)
- [ ] Dishwasher connected properly with no leaks
- [ ] Water supply to refrigerator connected (if applicable)
- [ ] Under-sink plumbing secure and leak-free
- [ ] Adequate water pressure at sink

### General Plumbing
- [ ] Water heater operational and properly installed
- [ ] Main water shut-off valve accessible and functioning
- [ ] No unusual noises in pipes when using fixtures
- [ ] Washing machine connections properly installed (if applicable)
- [ ] Water meter functioning and readable
- [ ] Water filter systems installed (if specified)

## Final Documentation Checklist

- [ ] Building completion certificate provided
- [ ] DEWA (Dubai Electricity and Water Authority) connection documents
- [ ] Property layout drawings and specifications

- [ ] Maintenance and operations manuals

- [ ] Keys, access cards, and remotes for all entry points

## Post-Inspection Process

After completing the inspection, follow these steps:

1. **Document all issues**: Take clear photographs of any defects
2. **Categorize by severity**: Prioritize items that affect safety or functionality
3. **Prepare a formal snagging list**: Send this to the developer in writing
4. **Set timelines**: Agree on when defects will be remedied
5. **Follow up**: Schedule a re-inspection after defects are supposedly fixed
6. **Final verification**: Conduct a final check before signing off on fixes

## Important Dubai-Specific Considerations

Dubai's unique environment and construction practices require special attention to:

- **Waterproofing**: Especially important given occasional heavy rains
- **Thermal insulation**: Crucial for energy efficiency in Dubai's climate
- **Window sealing**: To prevent dust infiltration during sandstorms
- **AC condensate drainage**: Properly routed to prevent water damage
- **Elevator access**: Ensure your access card works for designated floors (in apartments)
