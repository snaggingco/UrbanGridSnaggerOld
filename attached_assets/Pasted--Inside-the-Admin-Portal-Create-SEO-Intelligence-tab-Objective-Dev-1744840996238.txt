

### 🔍 **Inside the Admin Portal – Create 'SEO Intelligence' tab**

#### 🧭 **Objective**
Develop an admin-side tool that allows in-depth SEO and paid ads competitive intelligence by analyzing our domain against direct competitors for a list of specified keywords. The module should also simulate controlled ad search behavior for real-world visibility benchmarking.

---

### 📌 **Feature Overview**

#### 1. **SEO Competitor Analysis**
Create a dashboard where the admin can:
- Input a target domain (our website)
- Provide a list of competitor domains
- Enter a custom list of keywords (manual input or from our keyword research tools)

The system should then:
- Perform a **comprehensive SEO audit** of the entered domain and compare it side-by-side with the competitor domains
- Analyze on-page SEO factors (title tags, meta descriptions, content structure, keyword usage)
- Evaluate off-page SEO (backlink profiles, domain authority, referring domains)
- Assess technical SEO (page speed, mobile-friendliness, structured data, core web vitals)
- Compare SERP positions for the specified keywords across all domains

#### 2. **Strategic SEO Recommendations**
Based on the audit:
- Generate a set of **actionable, prioritized recommendations** that will help the admin understand what specifically needs to be done to **outperform competitors**
- Highlight keyword gaps, backlink opportunities, content quality differences, and technical issues that require resolution
- Include severity or impact scoring to aid prioritization (e.g., High/Medium/Low impact)

#### 3. **Simulated Ad Behavior Monitoring**
- Accept a list of keywords and trigger **search simulations** using an internal browser mechanism
- For each keyword, **perform a Google search** and:
  - Detect and **click on sponsored ads** in the search results
  - Limit activity to **10 keyword searches per hour** (configurable throttle to avoid abuse or bot detection)
  - Randomize behavior slightly (e.g., delay between clicks, scrolling, hover) to mimic human behavior
- Provide a log of:
  - Ads clicked (domain, time clicked, ad title)
  - Total clicks performed per keyword per hour
  - Detected changes in ad listings over time

---

### ✅ **Expected Outcomes**
- Gain full visibility into **how we rank vs competitors** across critical keywords
- Identify **SEO gaps and quick wins** to outperform competition
- Monitor **who’s bidding** on our keywords and simulate **real user interaction** to measure ad positioning and presence
- Use this data to **optimize both organic and paid campaigns**
