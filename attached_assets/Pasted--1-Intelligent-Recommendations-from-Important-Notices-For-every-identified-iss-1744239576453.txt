

---

### ✅ 1. Intelligent Recommendations from Important Notices

> For every identified issue or flag shown in the **"Important Notices"** section:
- Provide **customized recommendations** (e.g., “Increase contribution by 2% from year 7 to 9”).
- Enable **one-click actionable changes** to apply that fix directly into the study.
- Recommendations should appear inline with the notice and be context-aware (based on shortfalls, timing issues, etc.).

---

### 🔁 2. Editable Annual Contribution Increase (%)

> Enable the **Annual Contribution Increase (%)** field to be **user-editable**:
- Allow manual input or slider for fine-tuning escalation rates.
- Changes should dynamically reflect in funding strategy and future cash flow.

---

### 🏗 3. Overall Score in Condition Survey

> Once the **asset condition registry** is completed:
- Prompt the user to **assign or calculate an overall condition score** for the project.
- The score can be based on weighted averages or severity logic.
- This should feed into both reporting and risk-level communication in the dashboard.

---

### ℹ️ 4. Hover-Based Definitions (Tooltips)

> Add **clear hover tooltips** for all major outputs in:
- **Condition Survey Summary**
- **Reserve Fund Study Summary**

Each tooltip should define:
- What the value means
- How it was calculated (basic logic)
- Why it's important for decision-making

Examples:
- **“Target Reserve Balance”** → “This is the projected balance needed at the end of the study to handle future repairs beyond the current period.”
- **“Annual Requirement”** → “The estimated yearly contribution required to meet future obligations.”

---

### 📅 5. Asset Replacement & Repair Schedule Tab

> Introduce a new tab in the **Reserve Fund Analysis** module:
- Display a **timeline view** or **table** of:
  - Assets scheduled for repair or replacement
  - Expected year of replacement
  - Date of last major service (if available)
  - Estimated cost and expected lifespan remaining
- Allow filtering by asset type, replacement year, cost range, etc.

---

- Option for collapsible/expandable section groups
- Option for sticky floating mode in scrollable pages
