Prompt:

Build a Reserve Fund Study engine that calculates the minimum required annual contributions needed to fully fund a project's capital repair and replacement costs over a multi-year planning horizon. The study should:

Ensure no negative fund balances at any point.

Account for interest gained on the reserve balance.

Account for inflation on future capital costs.

Determine (not input) the target reserve balance required at the end of the period.

Provide options for different funding strategies, including flat and escalating contributions.

🎯 Goal:
Calculate the annual amount that needs to be collected and contributed to the reserve fund each year to ensure future capital expenditures are covered.

📥 Inputs (User-Entered):
study_period (int): Number of years (e.g., 30)

start_balance (decimal): Initial reserve balance in escrow (e.g., 50,000)

interest_rate (decimal): Annual interest rate earned on the reserve fund (e.g., 0.03 = 3%)

inflation_rate (decimal): Annual inflation rate for capital costs (e.g., 0.025 = 2.5%)

capital_plan (list of dict): List of capital events with fields:

year (int): Year of expense

amount (decimal): Estimated cost in today’s dollars

contribution_strategy (enum): "flat" (equal every year), "escalating" (e.g., 2% yearly increase)
