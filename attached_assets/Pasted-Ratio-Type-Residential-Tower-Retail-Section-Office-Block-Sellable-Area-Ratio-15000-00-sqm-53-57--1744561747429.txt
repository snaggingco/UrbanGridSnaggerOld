Ratio Explanations:

Sellable Area Ratio: Based on the proportion of each entity's sellable area to the total sellable area.
Applicable Area Ratio: Based on suit area and 25% of balcony area.
Dedicated Common Area Ratio: Proportion of each entity's dedicated common area to the total dedicated common area.
Parking Bay Area Ratio: Proportion of each entity's parking bay area to total parking bay area.
Total Component Area Ratio: Based on the sum of sellable area, parking bay area, dedicated common area, principal common area share, and common element area share.
Principal Common Area Ratio: Entity's share of principal common areas, distributed proportionally based on sellable area.
Common Element Area Ratio: Entity's share of common element areas, distributed proportionally based on sellable area.
Total Common Area Ratio: Sum of dedicated common area, proportional share from principal common areas, and proportional share from common element common areas.
